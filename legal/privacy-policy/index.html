Hosting and security

Cloudflare handles incoming traffic for delivery and security. Our web server records technical request data such as IP address, time, path, status and user agent to operate and protect the service. The service also uses IP-based security checks to detect proxy and VPN traffic.

We process these data for our legitimate interests in operating the service securely and troubleshooting it under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Discord sign-in and access

Discord provides your account ID, username and display name for sign-in. The portal checks your staff role with Discord and your Premium status against Disc-Tools records. The account menu displays your Discord avatar, which your browser loads from Discord’s CDN. Sign-in and access checks provide the requested portal features under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Cookies and browser storage

__Host-developer-oauth-state secures the login flow for up to 10 minutes. __Host-developer-session keeps you signed in for up to eight hours or until sign-out. Browser storage remembers which sidebar groups you expand and whether you dismissed the cookie notice. You can clear these preferences in your browser’s site settings.

API keys and activity

The portal stores a hash of each key, its name, account association, creation date, expiry and last use. Revoking a key deletes its key record. The Link Cleaner processes the URL you submit to return the cleaned result. Portal request logs record the key ID and name, account ID, request ID, time, status and processing time. These logs do not include the submitted URL or the key secret.

The portal shows request activity for up to 30 days. This is the display window. Request records support usage reporting and troubleshooting. Contact us for details about current storage and deletion practices. We process these data to provide API access and usage information under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and to troubleshoot and protect the service under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
